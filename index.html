What I'd do with you

Progress before headcount.

Some of the most valuable work happens before a company knows which engineer, agency, or full-time hire it needs.

01

Make sense of the messy problem

Clarify the outcome, identify assumptions, challenge the obvious approach and make practical tradeoffs.

02

Build the smallest decisive experiment

Use AI tools, rapid prototypes, existing technology and specialist help to test the riskiest question first.

03

Show what worked—and what didn't

Independent tests, useful artifacts, explicit unknowns and a clear decision about what to build next.

04

Turn results into a commercial next step

A product brief, an executable demonstration, a market test, or a concrete handoff to engineering.

My working philosophy
“I don't need to pretend I know everything. I need to know how to find out—and how to tell when the answer is actually good.”

Best fit: ambitious founders with a commercially meaningful problem, a willingness to test assumptions, and no patience for busywork. I can work fractionally or on a tightly scoped paid sprint.

Before the proofs

I've been on the founder side.

Products, customers, distribution, revenue and the pressure to make decisions with imperfect information.

My earlier work includes founding consumer mobile products (Feel Free Apps), AppyTaps, growth and performance marketing, and senior technical product marketing. I later founded Metalogic Labs to explore a different question: how to make AI-generated outcomes independently checkable.

That combination is what I bring to a founder: an instinct for the commercial problem, curiosity about the technical possibility, and a willingness to get hands-on with evidence.
